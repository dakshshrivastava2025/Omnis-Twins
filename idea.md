The idea
Omnis Twin is a software-only 3D Digital Twin that turns any smartphone into a predictive vehicle diagnostic platform with zero hardware. Using edge PINNs, acoustic CNNs, and RAG AI, it runs on phone sensors to detect faults, estimate component RUL, and visualize wear on an interactive 3D model.
 

What problem are you solving?
Traditional vehicle diagnostics rely on reactive check-engine lights and expensive physical OBD-II dongles, warning drivers only after mechanical failure occurs. Omnis Twin solves this by providing hardware-free, real-time predictive health monitoring and component degradation tracking directly on any smartphone before costly breakdowns happen.
